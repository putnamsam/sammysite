Fifty,
en route.

Nineteen days. London to Paris to Rome, then nowhere at all — just a lagoon, a retractable roof, and the stars.

SEA→ LHR→ CDG→ FCO→ MLE
Days 1–3 London Days 3–5 Paris Days 5–7 Rome Days 7–11 Maldives
Chapter One · London
Days 1–3

Art Deco and afternoon light

Qatar Airways QSuite from Seattle via Doha. Two nights at Claridge's, Mayfair.

See

  • Wallace Collection — free, uncrowded Old Masters
  • Kensington Palace — royal apartments and gardens
  • Sir John Soane's Museum — eccentric, intimate, free

Eat

  • Core by Clare Smyth — 3 Michelin stars
  • Sketch — Michelin-starred, theatrical rooms
  • Scott's Mayfair — classic seafood, old-school glamour
Chapter Two · Paris
Days 3–5

The postcard version, by rail

Eurostar Business Premier, St Pancras to Gare du Nord. Two nights at Hôtel Plaza Athénée.

See

  • Musée de l'Orangerie — Monet's Water Lilies, calm
  • Musée Rodin — sculpture garden
  • Sainte-Chapelle — stained-glass, breathtaking light

Eat

  • Épicure at Le Bristol — 3 Michelin stars
  • Guy Savoy — 3 Michelin stars, Left Bank
  • Le Jules Verne — inside the Eiffel Tower
Chapter Three · Rome
Days 5–7

The Secret Garden

Short flight from Paris. Two nights at Hotel de Russie, between the Spanish Steps and Piazza del Popolo.

See

  • Vatican Museums early-access tour — private, before the crowds
  • Galleria Borghese — Bernini, timed-entry
  • Trastevere evening walk — self-guided, no ticket needed

Eat

  • La Pergola — Rome's only 3-Michelin-star, rooftop
  • Le Jardin de Russie — in-hotel, the Secret Garden itself
  • Aroma at Palazzo Manfredi — Michelin-starred, Colosseum view
Chapter Four · Soneva Jani, Maldives
Days 7–11

The point of all this

Qatar Airways QSuite via Doha. Four nights, 1 Bedroom Water Retreat with Slide — retractable roof, private pool, straight into the lagoon.

Do

  • Sunset dolphin cruise — canapés and champagne
  • Private sandbank dinner — candlelit, own islet
  • Coral planting excursion — hands-on, low-key

Eat

  • So Starstruck — overwater observatory restaurant, book resort-side
  • Gizzi's — Thai-Asian, overwater
  • In-villa chef's table — arranged through Soneva reservations
